# HUMAN Decision — SPEC-016 G1

**Decision date:** 2026-09-15

**G1:** HUMAN APPROVED

**SPEC-016:** GOOGLE ADSENSE MONETIZATION INTEGRATION BOUNDARY APPROVED

**Planning baseline:** `27659a80bc5971fdc91dc2ea45578b153c460305`

The HUMAN approves creation and planning of SPEC-016 for Google AdSense as a secondary monetization channel. Affiliate conversion remains the primary business priority. The initial advertising strategy is manual, conservative, and limited to explicitly allowlisted ad slots; Auto Ads are not initially approved.

The approved boundary includes public/business/content/privacy readiness, ownership-verification infrastructure, configuration-driven and disabled-by-default capability, environment/hostname isolation, narrowly verified CSP requirements, real authorized `ads.txt` data only, a centralized public loader, reusable manual slots, conservative placement, accessibility, responsive behavior, performance budgets, kill switch, Preview validation, and a Production capability that remains disabled.

Stage 0 readiness is mandatory before advertising code. It must resolve truthful identity and contacts, audience and served regions, LGPD/legal review, personalization mode, CMP/consent strategy, privacy disclosures, original-content quality, image/text rights, final domain, eligible/denied routes, and a reproducible performance baseline. Missing HUMAN information must remain missing and blocking; it must not be fabricated.

AdSense reporting/API, OAuth, financial-data ingestion or persistence, Admin Monetization reporting, consolidated affiliate/advertising revenue, and all fabricated financial metrics are excluded and require a separate future SPEC.

This decision authorizes `SPEC-016`, `PLAN-016`, this G1 record, necessary DAG/governance documentation, staged checkpoints, acceptance criteria, and documentation consistency checks only. It does not authorize an implementation branch, AdSense scripts or slots, `ads.txt`, CSP changes, CMP/dependencies, environment variables, Publisher IDs, account/site-review operations, Vercel/Supabase changes, deployment, PR, merge, or Production activation.
